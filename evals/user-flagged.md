# Bộ test: câu người dùng đã chê

Every item is a sentence or paragraph a native reviewer rejected (or accepted as flagged) in Oct 2026. Use it after each change to the skill: give a fresh agent, with the skill, the **facts** and **context** only (never the rejected wording), ask it to write the line; then show a native reader the old line and the new one side by side. The reader judges; a model's score is not proof (SKILL § Who judges). Record the verdicts at the bottom with the date. Narration items (U08–U16) come from a history narration series on Hồ Chí Minh's years abroad; "ch.N" is its chapter.

Mode B (rewrite): give the rejected wording and ask for a rewrite. Mode A (write from facts) is the harder test, since the leak happens while composing.

Paragraph mode: since 2026-10-27, narration items are run as a whole paragraph, never as lone sentences, with the context column given as what the listener already heard (the previous chapter's points and last sentence) and what comes next. Lone sentences hid the real fault: each was fine, but the paragraph did not link (U15).
